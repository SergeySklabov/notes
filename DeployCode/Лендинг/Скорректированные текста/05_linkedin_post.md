**The Infrastructure Gap nobody talks about**

There's a well-known progression for SaaS companies:

**Stage 1** — Validate fast. Replit, Lovable, no-code. MVP in 2–3 days, $20–50/mo. Perfect for hypothesis testing.

**Stage 3** — Enterprise-grade. VPC, WAF, compliance, platform team, 50+ developers. IDP, Kubernetes, the whole nine yards.

But what about the space **in between?**

We mapped this journey using a real SaaS example (document automation) and found a consistent pattern. Companies with:
- $20K–$200K MRR
- 10–50 developers
- Growing security requirements from enterprise clients
- No budget for a dedicated platform team

These companies hit a painful gap:
- **PaaS is too abstract.** No VPC, no network control, no audit logs. Enterprise clients walk away.
- **IDP is too heavy.** 6–12 months to build, requires a platform team they can't afford.
- **DIY Terraform turns into chaos.** Writing a script is easy. Maintaining it for 3 years, onboarding people, standardizing environments — that's the hard part.

The trigger for this transition is predictable: infrastructure costs exceed 20–25% of MRR, clients demand SLA transparency, and the architecture hits platform limits.

I believe there's an emerging category here — an infrastructure layer for growing SaaS companies that need cloud-native control without building an IDP from scratch.
